## Overview（概述）

William Strunk Jr. 的 *The Elements of Style*（1918）教你清晰写作并无情删减。将这些规则应用于用户请求的任务。

## I. Introductory（引言）

本手册总结了 plain English style 的 essentials。它专注于最常被违反的用法规则和写作原则，为详尽手册提供了一个 compact 替代。先掌握这里的指导，然后向最好的作者学习更精细的风格要点。

## II. Elementary Rules Of Usage（基本用法规则）

### Rule 1. 单数名词所有格加 's

不管最后一个辅音是什么。因此写：

Charles's friend
Burns's poems
the witch's malice

例外：以 *-es* 和 *-is* 结尾的古专有名词的所有格、*Jesus'* 的所有格，以及 *for conscience' sake*、*for righteousness' sake* 等形式。

代词所有格 *hers*、*its*、*theirs*、*yours* 和 *oneself* 没有撇号。

### Rule 2. 三个或更多项目的系列中，除最后一个外每个术语后加逗号

因此写：

red, white, and blue
gold, silver, or copper

这也是 Government Printing Office 和 Oxford University Press 的用法。

### Rule 3. 插入语用逗号括起来

The best way to see a country, unless you are pressed for time, is to travel on foot.

### Rule 4. 引导并列从句的连词前加逗号

The early records of the city have disappeared, and the story of its first years can no longer be reconstructed.

### Rule 5. 不要用逗号连接独立从句

两个或更多语法完整且未用连词连接的从句要形成单个复合句时，正确的标点符号是分号。

Stevenson's romances are entertaining; they are full of exciting adventures.

### Rule 6. 不要把句子拆成两半

换句话说，不要用句号代替逗号。

I met them on a Cunard liner several years ago. Coming home from Liverpool to New York.

这两个例子中，第一个句号应改为逗号，后面的词小写开头。

### Rule 7. 句首的分词短语必须指代语法主语

Walking slowly down the road, he saw a woman accompanied by two children.

*Walking* 指句子的主语，而非 the woman。如果作者希望它指 the woman，必须重构句子。

## III. Elementary Principles Of Composition（基本写作原则）

### Rule 8. 以段落为写作单位：一个段落一个主题

### Rule 9. 通常以主题句开头，结尾与开头一致

### Rule 10. 使用主动语态

主动语态通常比被动语态更直接、更有力：

I shall always remember my first visit to Boston.

这比

My first visit to Boston will always be remembered by me.

好得多。后者更 indirect、更大胆、更 concise。

### Rule 11. 以肯定形式陈述

做出明确断言。避免 tame、colorless、hesitating、non-committal 的语言。只有在表示否认或对照时才使用 *not*。

| Original | Revision |
| --- | --- |
| He was not very often on time. | He usually came late. |
| not honest | dishonest |
| not important | trifling |
| did not remember | forgot |

### Rule 12. 使用明确、具体、具象的语言

Prefer the specific to the general, the definite to the vague, the concrete to the abstract.

| Original | Revision |
| --- | --- |
| A period of unfavorable weather set in. | It rained every day for a week. |
| He showed satisfaction as he took possession of his well-earned reward. | He grinned as he pocketed the coin. |

### Rule 13. 省略 needless words（needless 的词）

有力的写作是 concise 的。一个句子不应包含不必要的词，一个段落不应包含不必要的句子，就像一幅画不应有不必要的线条，一台机器不应有不必要的零件。

| Original | Revision |
| --- | --- |
| the question as to whether | whether |
| there is no doubt but that | no doubt |
| he is a man who | he |
| in a hasty manner | hastily |
| this is a subject which | this subject |

### Rule 14. 避免连续的松散句子

### Rule 15. 以相似形式表达并列思想

这是 parallel construction（平行结构）原则，要求相似内容和功能的表达 outwardly similar。

| Original | Revision |
| --- | --- |
| Formerly, science was taught by the textbook method, while now the laboratory method is employed. | Formerly, science was taught by the textbook method; now it is taught by the laboratory method. |

### Rule 16. 将相关的词放在一起

句子中词的位置是展示它们关系的主要手段。因此，作者必须尽可能将思想相关的词和词组放在一起，将不相关的分开。

| Original | Revision |
| --- | --- |
| Wordsworth, in the fifth book of The Excursion, gives a minute description of this church. | In the fifth book of The Excursion, Wordsworth gives a minute description of this church. |
| He wrote three articles about his adventures in Spain, which were published in Harper's Magazine. | He published in Harper's Magazine three articles about his adventures in Spain. |

### Rule 17. 总结中保持同一时态

### Rule 18. 将强调的词语放在句末

作者希望最突出的词或词组通常应在句末。

| Original | Revision |
| --- | --- |
| Humanity has hardly advanced in fortitude since that time, though it has advanced in many other ways. | Humanity, since that time, has advanced in many other ways, but it has hardly advanced in fortitude. |
